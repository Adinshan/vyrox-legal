1. Who operates VYROX

VYROX is an independently operated Discord application (“VYROX,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at brke5335@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

Depending on the commands, moderation features, and server settings in use, VYROX may process the following categories of information received through Discord:

  • Discord identifiers: Discord user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, and message IDs where needed for a feature.
  • Basic profile and interaction information: usernames, display names, command or interaction details, and information needed to identify the person using a command or affected by a moderation action.
  • Server configuration: settings selected by server administrators, such as configured channels, roles, moderation options, and feature preferences.
  • Moderation records: warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, action type, timestamps, the acting moderator's ID, the affected user's ID, and any reason or evidence entered or captured by the enabled feature.
  • Message information: message content may be processed if a moderation or other enabled feature needs it to perform its stated function. Whether any message content or excerpts are stored, and for how long, must be confirmed against the deployed bot code and configuration before publication. Do not claim that message content is or is not stored until this has been checked.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: error reports and operational logs generated by the bot or its hosting environment, which may contain identifiers or command details.

VYROX does not need your Discord password or login token to operate. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, payment card details, or other sensitive information to the bot.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Run VYROX commands and provide its server utility and moderation features.
  • Apply server configuration and permissions selected by authorized administrators.
  • Create and display moderation records, where those features are enabled.
  • Protect the bot, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and maintain service security.
  • Respond to privacy requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use Discord API data to build advertising profiles, sell personal information, or contact users outside Discord for marketing.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed or disclosed in these limited circumstances:

  • Discord: VYROX operates through Discord's platform and API. Discord processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Infrastructure providers: The bot is hosted by Orihost. The database or other persistent storage provider has not yet been identified. Before publishing, identify the actual database/storage service (if any) and name it here; do not assume Orihost is also the database provider. Also disclose any other services that receive VYROX data.
  • Server administrators: Moderation information may be visible to authorized moderators or administrators through VYROX commands, configured channels, or Discord permissions.
  • Legal and safety reasons: Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

Service providers may process information only to provide services to us and subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable requirements. We do not authorize them to use VYROX data for their own unrelated purposes.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep information only for as long as it is needed for the feature or purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements. Routine moderation records are intended to be retained for up to 90 days from creation, then deleted or irreversibly anonymized, except where a longer period is required by law or reasonably necessary for security or dispute resolution. This 90-day period applies to moderation records only; it does not establish a retention period for server configuration, technical logs, or backups. Set and document actual retention periods for those separately before publishing.

When VYROX is removed from a server, associated stored data will be deleted when it is no longer needed to provide the service, subject to applicable legal obligations and any separately disclosed backup or security retention periods. The 90-day moderation-record period continues to apply unless deletion is completed earlier. Users or server administrators may request deletion by emailing brke5335@gmail.com and including the relevant server ID and, where applicable, user ID. Do not include passwords or authentication tokens.

We will review requests and take action within a reasonable period and as required by applicable law. Some information may need to be retained temporarily for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance; where applicable, it will be restricted and deleted when no longer required.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to stored information should be limited to people and systems that need it to operate VYROX.

7. International processing

VYROX and its infrastructure providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can also stop using VYROX by removing it from your server. To submit a request, contact brke5335@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request and may be unable to fulfill requests that conflict with legal obligations or the rights of others.

9. Children's privacy

VYROX is not intended for anyone who is below the minimum age required to use Discord in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information to VYROX inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Third-party services and links
